Groups, combined with ACLs, give you the ability to control access to your objects among precise sets of users (these dimensions of access include reading, writing, and modifying ACLs.) You can define groups here, and click on them to add members. To change these groups' access to particular objects, simply create the appropriate access rule in the object's ACL editor, which appears in the "owner" menu when viewing the object.
