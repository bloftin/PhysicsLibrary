Velocity is tangent to the circle. Acceleration points toward its center.

Model. A point moves counterclockwise at constant speed on a circle. ω = v/R, T = 2π/ω, and ac = v2/R. Mass changes the required inward net force, not the kinematics. “Centripetal” describes the direction of that net force; it is not an additional force.
