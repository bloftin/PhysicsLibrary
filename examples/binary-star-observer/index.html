Model & Julia source

Keplerian, spherical detached stars with no third light, spots, reflection, tidal distortion or Doppler beaming. Stellar colors are labels, not temperatures.

Earth sees projected X–Y disks along fixed +Z. Inspection-camera motion does not change that observer. The unresolved view represents combined photometric light, not resolved telescope imaging.

Quadratic intensity: I(μ) / I(1) = 1 − 0.4(1 − μ) − 0.25(1 − μ)². The browser interpolates a precomputed Transits.jl kernel; uniform disks use exact overlap geometry.

Probability boundaries maximize allowed |cos i| over each half-orbit, rather than approximating conjunction as exactly edge-on. The two events share the same inclination.

Timing, eclipse duration and depth do not uniquely determine a binary without additional constraints.
