Model & Julia source

Ideal Keplerian two-body orbit. The observer is fixed along +Z; inspection-camera motion does not change the physical inclination. The sky plane is X–Y. ω rotates the relative separation orbit, not the inspection camera.

RV1 = γ + K1[cos(ν + ω) + e cos ω]. RV2 has the opposite orbital term. Wavelengths use the low-velocity optical convention λ = λ0(1 + RV/c).

One schematic Gaussian absorption line per visible component, convolved with a Gaussian instrument of FWHM c/R. SB1 suppresses secondary absorption, not secondary continuum. Mode selection is a model assumption; blending and real detectability are separate issues.

No eclipses, atmosphere synthesis, spots, tidal distortion, Rossiter–McLaughlin effect, precision barycentric corrections or relativistic effects. Colors identify stars, not their temperatures. This is a noiseless forward model, not a fit to real spectra.
