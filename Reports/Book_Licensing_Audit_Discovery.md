## Licensing evidence in rendered entry files

The snapshot contains rendered encyclopedia/exposition entry files. Many generated `.tex` files carry the standard header `PlanetPhysics is released under the GNU Free Documentation License.` This is evidence for those rendered entries, **not automatically for separately uploaded books**. Book objects therefore require title-level source/license verification rather than blanket assignment of the site GFDL.
