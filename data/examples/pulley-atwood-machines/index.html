Model. One massless, inextensible rope; frictionless, massless pulleys; uniform gravity; taut rope and release from rest. The movable system includes an ideal fixed redirecting pulley above the free mass. Positive display coordinates point downward for both masses. The load acceleration metric points upward when positive.
